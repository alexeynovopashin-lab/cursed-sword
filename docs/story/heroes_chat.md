## Task

Bring the swordsman (Габриель / Габриэла), the archer (Ивар / Эллион) and the
mage (Луциан / Луциания) to the level of Nobby — `docs/story/bible.md` §5,
paragraph «Нобби»: a concrete image with hooks for the voice. For each hero:
- look: height, face, clothes, one thing a player remembers;
- manner of speech: favourite words, how he or she jokes (voice only — this
  is what players hear);
- habits and quirks, what he or she likes and hates;
- a detail from the past that plays in the season (link to `season1.md`);
- male and female forms: every line works for both.

Order: one hero at a time, swordsman → archer → mage. Offer 2–3 concrete
variants per open point (one pro, one con, your pick), not open questions.
